Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Chronica is designed to help you document your life, reflect on what matters, and see your journey over time. We respect your privacy and aim to be clear about what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Who We Are

Chronica is a life documentation and reflection application operated by A.G. Strategic Partners Ltd.

For privacy-related questions, requests, or concerns, you can contact us at:

Email: privacy@chronica.so

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Chronica, we may collect and process the following categories of information.

Account Information

  • Email address
  • Account credentials and authentication information
  • Profile information that you choose to provide

Life and Reflection Information

Chronica allows you to document your life and personal experiences. This may include information you voluntarily enter into the app, such as:

  • Daily intentions
  • Daily reflections
  • Achievements
  • Life-day entries
  • Weekly reflections
  • Other information you voluntarily choose to record

Please remember that information you enter into Chronica may be personal or sensitive. You should only record information that you are comfortable storing within the service.

Subscription Information

If you subscribe to a paid Chronica plan, subscription and purchase information may be processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not need to receive or store your full payment-card details to provide the subscription service.

Technical Information

We may process technical information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Chronica. This can include information associated with your device, application version, operating system, network requests, and technical error information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use information we collect for purposes including:

  • Creating and maintaining your Chronica account
  • Providing the Chronica service and its features
  • Saving and displaying your life documentation
  • Calculating life progress and streak information
  • Providing reflections and insights
  • Processing and maintaining subscriptions
  • Sending service-related notifications when enabled
  • Maintaining the security and integrity of the service
  • Diagnosing technical problems and improving reliability
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

4. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Chronica uses secure connections and authentication mechanisms when transmitting information between the application and our services.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Personal Entries

Your daily entries and reflections are part of the core Chronica experience. Chronica is designed around preserving your documented journey over time.

When you delete your Chronica account, your account and associated personal data are permanently deleted from our active systems. This includes your documented life data, such as intentions, reflections, achievements, life-day entries, weekly reflections, account records, and other data associated with your account.

Deletion may take additional time for data contained in backups or where retention is required by law. Where information must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes, it will be retained only for as long as necessary for that purpose and handled in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

If you have a privacy or deletion request concerning your personal information, contact us at privacy@chronica.so. Account deletion requests are processed in accordance with the deletion process described above and applicable legal requirements.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or disclose information only where reasonably necessary to operate Chronica, provide requested services, protect the service, or comply with legal obligations.

This may include carefully selected service providers that assist us with hosting, infrastructure, authentication, notifications, subscriptions, security, or other technical operations.

Such providers may process information only as necessary to perform their services for us and subject to appropriate contractual or security safeguards where applicable.

7. App Store and Google Play

Chronica may be distributed through Apple's App Store and Google's Google Play Store.

Apple and Google may independently process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms. Purchases made through those platforms are subject to the applicable platform's payment and subscription systems.

8. Notifications

Chronica may offer notifications to help you maintain your daily practice.

You can control notification permissions through your device settings. Service-related notifications may also be sent where necessary to operate your account or provide requested functionality.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Chronica, operate and secure the service, fulfill our legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and protect against fraud or misuse. When you delete your account, associated personal data is permanently deleted from our active systems, subject to any information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information. These may include rights to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of personal information where applicable
  • Request restriction of certain processing
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request a copy of certain information in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise an applicable privacy right, contact: privacy@chronica.so.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use the service. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Depending on where you live and where our service providers operate, your information may be processed or stored in countries other than your country of residence.

Where required by applicable law, we will use appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal information.

13. Third-Party Services

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

When we make material changes, we will take reasonable steps to notify users where appropriate. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
